Profiles and routines

How do I add a child profile?

Tap the Children tab, then tap the plus button in the top-right corner. Enter the profile details and choose an avatar emoji and color. You can also select an optional photo.

How do I create a routine?

Open the Routines tab and tap the plus button. Start with a starter pack, browse individual age-appropriate templates, or build a custom routine.

What is Kid Mode?

Kid Mode is a supervised, one-task-at-a-time view with large controls and completion animations. A parent, guardian, or caregiver configures the routine and can exit Kid Mode using the tap-and-hold or PIN method selected in Settings.

How do stars and rewards work?

A completed routine task can earn a star. Caregivers can create rewards, set their star costs, and help a child redeem earned stars. The feature is intended as an optional organizational and motivational tool.

Free and Premium

What is included in Free?

Free includes up to two profiles, two routines per profile, six routine templates, Kid Mode, stars and rewards, and progress tracking. Premium adds unlimited profiles, unlimited routines, all 34 templates, and full starter packs.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and select Restore Purchases. StoreKit asks Apple to restore active purchases associated with the Apple Account being used on the device. An internet connection may be required.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your device, select your Apple Account, then select Subscriptions and Little Routines. Apple handles billing, plan management, and cancellation.

Privacy and local app content

What stays on my device?

Profile details, routines, tasks, progress, stars, rewards, and optional user-selected photos are stored as local app content. Little Routines does not send that profile, routine, or photo content to Tyche Ventures. Depending on your device settings, Apple-managed backups may include a copy of local app data.

Can Little Routines see my photo library?

No. Apple’s system photo picker lets you choose a specific photo without giving Little Routines access to browse your full photo library. A photo you choose becomes part of the app’s local content.

Does the app contain ads or analytics?

No. Little Routines does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. StoreKit still communicates with Apple when you view, purchase, verify, or restore a subscription.

Using Little Routines offline

Core profile, routine, Kid Mode, progress, star, and reward features can be used without an internet connection. A connection is needed for App Store and StoreKit activity such as downloading updates, loading current subscription information, making a purchase, or restoring purchases.
