Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my child? Tap the "Children" tab at the bottom, then tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Enter your child's name, date of birth, and choose an avatar emoji and color.
How do I create a routine? Go to the "Routines" tab and tap "+". You can choose from starter packs (like Daily Essentials or Morning Routine) or browse individual age-appropriate templates. You can also create fully custom routines.
What is Kid Mode? Kid Mode lets your child complete their routines independently with big, tappable buttons and fun animations. Stars are earned for each completed task. Parents exit Kid Mode by tapping and holding the exit button or entering a PIN code (configurable in Settings).
How do stars and rewards work? Children earn stars by completing routine tasks. You can set up custom rewards (like "Extra screen time" or "Pick dinner") with star costs. Kids can redeem their earned stars for these rewards — making routines fun and motivating!
What's included in the free version? The free version includes up to 2 children, 2 routines per child, 5 routine templates, and basic progress tracking. Premium unlocks unlimited children, unlimited routines, all 24 templates, Kid Mode, rewards, and more.
How do I restore my subscription? Go to the premium screen (try adding a 3rd child or 3rd routine) and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom. This will restore any active subscriptions linked to your Apple ID.
Is my data safe? Yes! All data is stored locally on your device only. We never collect, transmit, or have access to your information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Do I need an internet connection? No. The app works completely offline. Internet is only needed for subscription purchases and restoring purchases.
How do I cancel my subscription? Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device to manage or cancel.
