Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built the Little Routines app (the "App") as a free application with optional premium features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

All data you enter in the App — including child profiles, names, ages, routines, tasks, progress, stars, and rewards — is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data.

2. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed to help parents and caregivers create daily routines for children. While the App stores information about children (names, ages, avatars), this information is:

  • Stored only on the user's device
  • Never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Never used for advertising or tracking
  • Fully under the user's control and can be deleted at any time

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws worldwide. Since we do not collect any data from children or adults, no parental consent mechanisms are required.

3. Kid Mode

The App includes a "Kid Mode" feature designed for children to interact with directly. In Kid Mode:

  • Children complete routine tasks by tapping checkmarks
  • No personal data is collected or transmitted
  • All progress and star data is stored locally on the device
  • Parents control Kid Mode access via tap-and-hold or PIN exit methods

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode/biometric security
  • Apple's built-in data encryption (Data Protection)
  • The App does not create any user accounts or cloud backups

If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

6. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, or any other activity within the App. We do not display advertisements.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. No data leaves your device.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App
  • Correction: You can edit any information at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records or use "Clear All Data" in Settings, or uninstall the App to remove all data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
