Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built the Little Routines app (the "App") as a free application with optional premium features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Age Requirement

The App is intended to be downloaded, configured, and managed by parents, guardians, and caregivers. It includes a supervised Kid Mode that an adult may let a child use. It is not designed for children to create accounts, communicate with others, make purchases, or use independently. We do not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

All data you enter in the App — including child profiles, names, ages, routines, tasks, progress, stars, and rewards — is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data. We do not have servers that receive, store, or process your information.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed to help parents and caregivers create daily routines for children. While the App stores information about children (names, ages, avatars), this information is:

  • Stored only on the user's device
  • Never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Never used for advertising, tracking, or profiling
  • Fully under the parent/guardian's control and can be deleted at any time

The App does not ask a child to create an account or submit personal information to us. A parent, guardian, or caregiver controls the information entered on the device and can edit or delete it at any time. If you believe information was sent to us in error, please contact us so we can investigate.

4. Kid Mode

The App includes a supervised "Kid Mode" that a parent, guardian, or caregiver may let a child use. The adult remains responsible for configuring the routine and supervising use. In Kid Mode:

  • Children complete routine tasks by tapping checkmarks
  • No personal data is collected or transmitted
  • All progress and star data is stored locally on the device
  • Parents control Kid Mode access via tap-and-hold or PIN exit methods

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode/biometric security (Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Apple's built-in data encryption (Data Protection)
  • The App does not create user accounts or provide its own cloud-sync service

Your device may include App data in backups managed through your Apple settings. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by us. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device, although an existing device backup may retain a copy according to Apple's backup practices.

7. Data Breach Notification

We do not operate servers or databases that receive App profile or routine data. This limits exposure through our systems but does not eliminate device, operating-system, or software security risks. If we discover an App vulnerability that could compromise locally stored data, we will address it through an App update and provide appropriate guidance.

8. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, location, or any other activity within the App. We do not display advertisements. We do not use cookies. We do not create user profiles.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. No data leaves your device through the App. We will never sell your data, and we have never sold user data.

10. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full and complete control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App at all times
  • Correction: You can edit any information at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records, use "Clear All Data" in Settings, or uninstall the App to remove all data permanently
  • Portability: Your data exists on your device and is accessible through the App

We do not receive the profile and routine information stored within the App, so we generally cannot access, correct, export, or delete it for you. You can manage that information directly in the App or remove it by deleting the App. If you have a privacy request or question about information you believe we may have received outside the App, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
