⭐ Little Routines

Visual daily routines for kids. Stars, rewards, and independence.

Little Routines helps kids build healthy habits with visual daily routines — morning, bedtime, after-school, and more. Children tap through tasks in Kid Mode, earn stars, and redeem rewards. Parents track progress and customize routines for each child. Simple, warm, and designed for everyday families. All data stays private on your device.
