Private by design

Your routine content is not sent to Tyche.

Profiles, routines, progress, rewards, and optional photos you select stay in the app’s local storage. No Tyche Ventures account is required.

Local app content

Routine and profile content is stored on your device. Apple-managed backups may include app data depending on your backup settings.

Photos are optional

You choose any profile or task photo through Apple’s system photo picker. Little Routines does not receive your photo library.

Apple handles purchases

StoreKit communicates with Apple for subscriptions, eligibility, purchase verification, and restoration. Tyche does not receive your full payment information.

No ads or analytics SDK

The app contains no advertising network or third-party behavioral analytics SDK. Read the privacy policy.
