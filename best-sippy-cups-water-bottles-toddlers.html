Getting a toddler to drink enough water throughout the day is one thing. Finding a cup that doesn't leak all over the diaper bag is another. We tested dozens of sippy cups and water bottles with kids ages 1–4 to find the ones that actually hold up — no leaks, no meltdowns, no mystery mold hiding in the valves.

What to Look For in a Toddler Cup or Water Bottle

  • Spill-proof design — because it will end up upside down in a car seat
  • Easy to drink from — straws, spouts, or 360° rims that don't require a physics degree
  • Few parts — more parts means more places for mold to hide
  • Durability — it's getting thrown off the high chair. Repeatedly.
  • Easy to clean — dishwasher-safe with parts that actually come apart
  • Size and grip — small enough for toddler hands, big enough to last a few hours

Tips for Keeping Toddlers Hydrated

  • Offer water with every meal and snack — don't wait for them to ask
  • Let them pick their own cup or bottle — ownership increases interest
  • Add a splash of fruit (frozen berries work great) to make water more exciting
  • Clean straw valves thoroughly at least once a week — a pipe cleaner is your best friend
  • Use an app like Kids Meal Planner to plan meals and snacks that include hydrating foods like watermelon, cucumber, and yogurt
