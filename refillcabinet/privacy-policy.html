Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

⚠️ RefillCabinet is an organizational tool only. It does not provide medical advice. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the sensitive health-adjacent information you store in the app.

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built the RefillCabinet app (the "App") as a free application with optional premium features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. Because RefillCabinet is used to organize medication-related information, we take your privacy with the utmost seriousness.

1. Age Requirement

The App is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). The App is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are under 18, do not use this App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, receive, or transmit any personal data or health-related data to our servers or any third-party servers. We have zero servers. We operate zero databases. We have zero ability to access your data.

We do not have access to your medication data. We cannot see, retrieve, or reconstruct it. Your data exists only on your physical device and nowhere else.

3. Types of Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App allows you to store the following types of information locally on your device. None of this data is ever transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Profile information: Names and roles (Self, Family Member, Caregiver) of household members
  • Medication names: Names of medications as entered by you
  • Dosage information: Dosage amounts, forms (tablet, capsule, liquid, etc.), and units
  • Scheduling data: Dose times, frequencies, and recurrence patterns
  • Dose logs: Records of doses taken, skipped, snoozed, or missed, with timestamps
  • Supply tracking data: Quantity on hand and refill threshold values
  • Notes: Any free-text notes you add to medications or profiles
  • App preferences: Notification settings and display preferences

All of this data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework locally on your device's storage. It is never uploaded, synced, transmitted, or made accessible to us or any third party through the App.

4. Health-Adjacent Data — Special Considerations

We recognize that medication names, dosages, schedules, and adherence logs constitute sensitive health-adjacent information. Even though we never access this data, we want to be transparent about its nature:

  • Medication data can reveal health conditions, treatment plans, and adherence patterns
  • Profile information combined with medication data could identify individuals and their health situations
  • This data is inherently sensitive and deserves the highest standard of privacy protection

Our approach to this sensitivity is simple and absolute: we never touch your data. It exists only on your device. We have architected the App with no capability — technical or otherwise — to access, collect, or transmit your health-adjacent information.

5. HIPAA Compliance Statement

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) applies to "covered entities" (healthcare providers, health plans, and healthcare clearinghouses) and their business associates. Tyche Ventures LLC is not a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA. We do not provide healthcare services, process health insurance claims, or operate as a healthcare clearinghouse.

However, we recognize the spirit and intent of HIPAA's privacy protections, and we treat your medication data with equivalent — and arguably greater — seriousness:

  • Data minimization: We collect zero data. HIPAA's "minimum necessary" standard is inherently satisfied because we access nothing.
  • Access controls: No one at Tyche Ventures LLC can access your data because it never leaves your device. There are no servers to breach, no databases to query, no logs to review.
  • Data integrity: Your data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and protected by your device's built-in encryption and access controls (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode).
  • No disclosure: We cannot disclose your health information to any party — including law enforcement, insurers, or employers — because we do not possess it.

While HIPAA does not legally bind us, our zero-collection architecture provides a level of privacy protection that exceeds HIPAA's requirements for data handling, because the most secure data is data that is never collected.

6. No Analytics, Tracking, or Advertising

We do not use any analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, advertising frameworks, crash reporters, or telemetry of any kind. Specifically:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar services
  • No Facebook SDK, Google Ads SDK, or any advertising network
  • No crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag)
  • No usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • No device fingerprinting or identifier collection
  • No cookies, web beacons, or tracking technologies
  • No A/B testing or experimentation frameworks

We do not know how many users we have, how often the App is used, which features are popular, or any other usage metrics. This is by design.

7. Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications for dose reminders and refill alerts. These notifications are:

  • Generated entirely on your device using iOS's local notification system
  • Not sent through any server — there is no push notification server, no Apple Push Notification service (APNs) involvement, and no third-party notification service
  • Scheduled locally based on the dose times and refill thresholds you configure
  • Manageable through your device's Settings → RefillCabinet → Notifications at any time

No notification content (including medication names or dose information) is ever transmitted off your device.

8. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple using StoreKit 2. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, billing address, Apple ID, or any transaction details beyond the subscription status (active/inactive) that Apple's StoreKit framework makes available locally on your device. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. PDF Export Feature

The App provides a PDF export feature that generates a medication list document on your device. This PDF is created locally and is shared only through methods you explicitly choose (e.g., AirDrop, email, Messages, printing). We never access, receive, or store your exported files. You are solely responsible for how and with whom you share exported medication information.

10. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID authentication
  • Apple's built-in Data Protection encryption (AES-256)
  • iOS sandboxing, which prevents other apps from accessing RefillCabinet's data
  • No user accounts, no cloud storage, no remote backups created by the App

If you delete the App, all associated data — including all medication names, dosages, schedules, dose logs, profiles, and notes — is permanently and irreversibly removed from your device. There is no way for us or anyone else to recover this data because it was never stored anywhere other than your device.

11. Data Breach Notification

Because all data is stored locally on your device and we do not operate servers, databases, cloud infrastructure, or any system that stores user information, the risk of a data breach from our systems is zero. We have no systems to breach.

The risk to your medication data is limited to risks involving your physical device:

  • Device theft or loss (mitigated by device passcode/biometric locks and Apple's encryption)
  • Unauthorized physical access to an unlocked device
  • Device malware or operating system vulnerabilities (mitigated by keeping iOS updated)

In the unlikely event that a vulnerability in the App itself is discovered that could compromise locally stored data, we will promptly:

  • Disclose the issue via an App update and on our website
  • Provide guidance on how to protect your information
  • Release a patched version of the App as quickly as possible

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, license, or otherwise share any user data with any third party. No data leaves your device through the App. We have never sold user data. We will never sell user data. We do not have user data to sell.

13. Third-Party Services

The App does not connect to any third-party services, APIs, or external systems. The only external interaction is:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit 2: For subscription purchases and management. This interaction is handled entirely by Apple's frameworks and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. No medication data or personal information is shared during this interaction.

There are no other third-party services, SDKs, frameworks, or network connections used by the App.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected about them
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising their privacy rights

Because we collect zero personal information and sell zero data, these rights are inherently and completely satisfied. There is no personal information for us to disclose, delete, or stop selling, because we never collected any in the first place.

15. Your Rights — GDPR (EU/EEA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), EU/EEA residents have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. Because:

  • We do not process any personal data
  • We do not store any personal data on our systems
  • All data remains on your device under your sole control

These rights are inherently satisfied. You have full control over all your data within the App at all times. You can view, edit, delete, or export your data without contacting us.

We do not have a Data Protection Officer because we do not process personal data. If you have questions about our data practices, contact us at the address in Section 20.

16. Your Rights — General

Regardless of your jurisdiction, you have full and complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App at all times
  • Correction: You can edit any information at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records, profiles, or medications, or uninstall the App to remove all data permanently
  • Portability: You can export medication lists as PDF using the built-in export feature

17. Data Retention

All data retention is entirely within your control. You decide what to keep, modify, or delete at any time through the App.

When you delete the App: All associated data — every medication name, dosage, schedule, dose log, profile, note, and preference — is permanently and irreversibly removed from your device. There is no server-side retention because no data ever leaves your device. There are no backups maintained by us. There are no copies of your data anywhere.

Once data is deleted, it cannot be recovered by us, by Apple, or by any third party. You are solely responsible for exporting and backing up any data you wish to preserve before deleting the App.

18. International Users

Because the App stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to any server, there are no cross-border data transfers to consider. Your data stays on your device regardless of where you are located.
