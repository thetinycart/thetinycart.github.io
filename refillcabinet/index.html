Everything You Need

👥

Household Profiles

Create profiles for yourself, family members, or people you care for. Each profile has its own medication list and schedule.

⏰

Dose Reminders

Set daily, weekly, interval, or as-needed schedules. Get local notifications when it's time to take a dose.

✅

Dose Logging

Log doses as taken, skipped, snoozed, or missed. Keep a complete history of medication adherence.

📦

Refill Tracking

Track supply on hand and set refill thresholds. Get alerted before you run out so you never miss a refill.

📄

PDF Export

Export your medication list as a clean PDF to share with healthcare providers or keep as a backup.

🔒

100% Private

All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your medication info is yours alone.

Your privacy is absolute. RefillCabinet stores everything locally on your device. We have zero servers, collect zero data, and have zero access to your medication information. Not now, not ever.

Important: RefillCabinet is a personal organizational tool only. It does not provide medical advice, verify dosages, check drug interactions, or replace professional healthcare guidance. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions. In case of emergency, call 911.
