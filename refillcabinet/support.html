Frequently Asked Questions

RefillCabinet helps you organize medication schedules, track doses, and manage refills for your household — all stored privately on your device.

How do I add a profile? On the main screen, tap the "+" button or go to Profiles. Enter the person's name and select a role (Self, Family Member, or Caregiver). Each profile has its own independent medication list and schedule. The free tier supports up to 3 profiles.
How do I add a medication? Navigate to a profile, then tap "Add Medication." Enter the medication name, dosage, form (tablet, capsule, liquid, etc.), and any notes. You can also set the current supply quantity and a refill threshold to receive low-supply alerts.
How do I schedule doses? When adding or editing a medication, tap "Schedule." You can choose from several frequency options:
  • Daily — same time(s) every day
  • Specific weekdays — choose which days of the week
  • Interval — every X days
  • As needed (PRN) — no automatic schedule; log when taken
You can set multiple dose times per day for each medication.
How do I log a dose? When a dose is due, you'll see it on the dashboard. Tap the dose to log it as Taken, Skipped, or Snoozed. If a dose time passes without being logged, it will be marked as Missed. You can also log past doses manually from the medication detail screen.
How does refill tracking work? For each medication, you can enter the quantity on hand (e.g., "30 tablets") and a refill threshold (e.g., "7 tablets"). When your logged doses reduce the supply below the threshold, RefillCabinet sends a local notification reminding you to refill. You can update the supply count manually after picking up a refill.
How do supply and refill alerts work? Refill alerts are triggered when your tracked supply drops below the threshold you set. These are local notifications generated entirely on your device — no server or pharmacy connection is involved. Make sure notifications are enabled in your device's Settings → RefillCabinet for alerts to appear.
Can I export my medication list? Yes! Go to a profile and tap "Export" to generate a PDF of the medication list, including names, dosages, schedules, and notes. You can share this PDF via AirDrop, email, Messages, or save it to Files. This is useful for sharing with healthcare providers or keeping a printed backup.
How do I back up my data? Your data is stored locally on your device. To protect against data loss, we recommend regularly exporting your medication lists as PDFs and saving them to a safe location. If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
What's included in the free version? The free version includes 1 household, up to 3 profiles, and up to 10 active medications. You get full scheduling, dose logging, refill tracking, notifications, and PDF export. Premium unlocks unlimited profiles and unlimited medications.
How do I manage my subscription? Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device to view, change, or cancel your RefillCabinet subscription. You can also tap "Restore Purchases" within the app if you need to restore a previously purchased subscription on a new device.
How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, find RefillCabinet, and tap Cancel. Your premium features will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
Is my data safe and private? Yes. All data — including medication names, dosages, profile names, and dose logs — is stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your information. There are no servers, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no tracking. Your medication data never leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do I need an internet connection? No. RefillCabinet works entirely offline. Internet is only needed for purchasing or restoring subscriptions through the App Store.
Why didn't I receive a dose reminder? Notifications depend on your device settings. Please check: (1) Settings → RefillCabinet → Notifications are enabled, (2) Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is not blocking alerts, (3) the app has not been force-quit (iOS may limit background notifications for force-quit apps). RefillCabinet uses local notifications only — there is no push notification server. Notification delivery is best-effort and depends on iOS system behavior.
Can I use this app for someone I care for? Yes. RefillCabinet supports a Caregiver profile role. You can add profiles for people you help manage medications for. However, please note that the app is an organizational tool only. Caregivers are solely responsible for all medication management decisions. The app does not verify dosages, check drug interactions, or provide medical guidance of any kind.
Does RefillCabinet check for drug interactions? No. RefillCabinet does not check for drug interactions, contraindications, allergies, or side effects. It is purely an organizational and reminder tool. Always consult your pharmacist or healthcare provider about potential interactions between medications.
