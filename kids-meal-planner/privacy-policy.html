Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built the Kids Meal Planner app (the "App") as a free application with optional premium features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

All data you enter in the App — including child profiles, names, ages, allergies, contact information, pantry items, meal schedules, and photos — is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data.

2. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed to help parents and caregivers plan meals for children. While the App stores information about children (names, ages, allergies), this information is:

  • Stored only on the user's device
  • Never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Never used for advertising or tracking
  • Fully under the user's control and can be deleted at any time

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws worldwide. Since we do not collect any data from children or adults, no parental consent mechanisms are required.

3. Camera and Photo Access

The App may request access to your device's camera and photo library for the following purposes:

  • Barcode Scanning: To scan product barcodes and auto-populate pantry item information
  • Photo Capture: To take photos of pantry items or children for profiles

Photos and camera data are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Open Food Facts API: When you scan a barcode, the App may send the barcode number to the Open Food Facts API (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product information (name, brand, category). No personal information is sent — only the barcode number. Open Food Facts is a free, open-source database. Their privacy policy is available at openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use.

Amazon Associates: The App contains links to products on Amazon.com using our Amazon Associates affiliate tag. When you tap a product link, you will be directed to Amazon's website or app. Amazon's privacy practices are governed by Amazon's Privacy Notice. We do not receive any personal information about your Amazon purchases — only aggregated, anonymous sales data.

5. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode/biometric security
  • Apple's built-in data encryption (FileVault/Data Protection)
  • The App does not create any user accounts or cloud backups

If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

6. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, or any other activity within the App. We do not display advertisements.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts (when you scan a product)
  • Content you explicitly choose to share (e.g., emailing a PDF schedule to a caregiver)

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App
  • Correction: You can edit any information at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records or uninstall the App to remove all data
  • Portability: You can export meal schedules as PDF files

9. Medical and Nutritional Disclaimer

The App does not verify allergy safety or provide medical or nutritional advice. The App is a planning tool only. Parents and caregivers are solely responsible for verifying food safety, checking for allergens, and consulting healthcare professionals regarding their children's dietary needs.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
