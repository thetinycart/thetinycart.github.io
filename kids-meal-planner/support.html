Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my child? Tap the "Children" tab at the bottom, then tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in your child's details, allergies, and contact information.
How do I stock my pantry quickly? Go to the "Pantry" tab and tap the ⚡ (lightning bolt) icon for Quick Add. You can select from 70+ common foods and meals with one tap.
How do I scan a barcode? In the Pantry tab, tap the barcode scanner icon in the top right. Point your camera at the product's barcode — the item details will auto-populate.
How do I share the meal schedule? Go to the Schedule tab and tap the export icon (square with arrow). You can generate a PDF to print, text, email, or AirDrop to caregivers.
Is my data safe? Yes! All data is stored locally on your device only. We never collect, transmit, or have access to your information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Does the app track allergies? Yes. You can add allergies with severity levels for each child. Allergies are prominently displayed on all PDF exports. However, the app does not verify food safety — parents and caregivers are responsible for checking allergens.
Can I use the app for multiple children? Yes! You can add multiple children, each with their own profile, allergies, and meal schedule.
Do I need an internet connection? No. The app works completely offline. Internet is only needed for barcode scanning (to look up product info) and Amazon product links.
How do I cancel my subscription? Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device to manage or cancel.
