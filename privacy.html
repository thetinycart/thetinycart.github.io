Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates The Tiny Cart website (tycheventuresllc.com) and develops iOS applications including Kids Meal Planner, Little Routines, and CartKind. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website, apps, or digital products.

1. Age Requirement

Our website and apps are intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). Our services are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Website (tycheventuresllc.com)

We do not collect any personal information from our website. This site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, user accounts, or any form of data collection. We do not have contact forms, newsletters, or other data collection mechanisms.

2.2 iOS Apps

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from our apps to our servers. All data you create in our apps is stored locally on your device only using Apple's SwiftData framework. We have no access to this data and do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your information.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Amazon Associates Program

This website is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, Amazon may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not have access to any of your Amazon account information, purchase history, or personal data.

3.2 GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details about their data practices.

3.3 Apple App Store / StoreKit

If you purchase subscriptions in our apps, transactions are handled entirely by Apple through their App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3.4 External APIs (App-Specific)

Some of our apps may query external APIs for functionality (such as Open Food Facts for ingredient information in CartKind). When this occurs, only necessary identifiers (like product barcodes) are transmitted — never personal information or device identifiers.

4. Data Storage and Security

For our iOS apps, all user data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode/biometric security (Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Apple's built-in data encryption (Data Protection)
  • No cloud backups or external synchronization (unless you enable iCloud backup on your device)

If you delete an app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device. We cannot recover deleted app data as we do not have access to it.

5. Data Breach Notification

Because we do not collect, store, or process personal data on our servers, the risk of a data breach from our systems is effectively zero. For our website, the only third-party data handling is through GitHub's hosting platform. For our apps, all data remains on your device. In the unlikely event that a vulnerability is discovered that could compromise user data, we will promptly disclose the issue via app updates, our website, and provide guidance on how to protect your information.

6. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising technologies. Our website and apps do not include:

  • Google Analytics or similar tracking services
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Advertising networks or personalized ads
  • User behavior tracking or profiling
  • Cookies or local storage for tracking purposes

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. However, since we do not collect, sell, or share personal information, these rights are not applicable to our services. We do not:

  • Sell personal information
  • Share personal information with third parties for their commercial purposes
  • Collect personal information for profiling or targeted advertising

7.2 European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For EU residents, since we do not process personal data, GDPR obligations regarding data processing, consent, and individual rights do not apply to our services. We do not transfer personal data outside the EU because we do not collect it.

8. International Data Transfers

Since we do not collect or process personal data, there are no international data transfers to consider. Our apps store data locally on your device, and our website does not collect personal information.

9. Data Retention

We do not retain personal data because we do not collect it. For our iOS apps, data persists locally on your device until you delete the app or manually delete the data. For our website, no personal data is stored or retained.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will post any updated Privacy Policy on this website with an updated "Last updated" date. For significant changes that may affect your privacy rights, we may provide additional notice through our website or app updates.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action to remove such information.
