How we build

Clear interfaces, honest limits, and fewer accounts.

Good software should make its boundaries as understandable as its features.

01 · FOCUSED

A defined job

Each app is organized around a specific real-world workflow instead of a maze of unrelated features.

02 · LOCAL-FIRST

Less account friction

Core planning and record data stays local whenever the product can reasonably work that way. Network features are disclosed where they occur.

03 · PLAIN LANGUAGE

No hidden certainty

Recall matches, barcode data, templates, and planning notes are presented as aids—not guarantees, diagnoses, or substitutes for official sources.
