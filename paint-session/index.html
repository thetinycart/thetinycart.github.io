Less time reconstructing. More time painting.

From a first idea to the next session.

Miniatures, landscapes, still lifes, color studies and more. Templates suggest what to document; they do not prescribe the right way to make art.

Your work stays yours.

Projects and templates are stored on the device where you create them. You choose when to export an editable project or template backup, share a PDF, or restore work from Recently Deleted. There is no automatic account or cross-device sync service.
