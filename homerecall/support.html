Frequently Asked Questions

HomeRecall is designed for homeowners and households that want to stay on top of maintenance, track what they own, and keep records organized across one or more properties.

How do I add a property?

On the Home tab, tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Enter your property name, address, and optionally add a photo. Your property will appear as a tile on the home screen.

How do I add assets (appliances, systems)?

Navigate to your property, then tap the Assets tab. Tap "+" and choose from the categorized list of common home assets (HVAC, plumbing, kitchen appliances, etc.) or add a custom asset. You can record the make, model, serial number, and warranty dates.

How do maintenance tasks work?

You can choose from suggested maintenance templates (e.g., "Replace HVAC Filter" every 3 months) or create custom tasks. Tasks can be one-time or recurring, and the app can send local reminders when they are due if you enable notifications.

How do I complete a task?

Tap an active task and choose a quick completion or completion with details. You can also swipe right on the task to reveal "Complete" and "Log Details." Completed recurring tasks automatically schedule the next occurrence.

Can I attach documents like warranties and receipts?

Yes! You can attach photos, scan documents, or import PDFs to any property or asset. Common types include receipts, warranties, manuals, inspection reports, and insurance documents.

How do I back up my data?

Go to More → Backup & Restore → Export. This creates a .homerecall archive containing your properties, assets, tasks, maintenance logs, document metadata, and included property or document files. Saved recall matches and recall-check history are not included; HomeRecall checks again after restore as you use the app, when internet access and the required asset information are available. HomeRecall does not encrypt exported archives, so save or share the file only through a destination you trust. To restore, use Import on the same screen.

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes 1 property, up to 5 assets with full maintenance templates, unlimited tasks, document attachments, and the export/backup feature. Premium unlocks unlimited properties and unlimited assets.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to More → tap "Upgrade to Premium" → tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom. This will restore any active subscriptions linked to your Apple ID.

Is my data safe?

HomeRecall is local-first: your property records, photos, documents, tasks, and saved results are stored in the app on your device, and no HomeRecall account is required. CPSC recall checks send asset brand and model search terms to SaferProducts.gov, optional map previews use Apple Maps, and subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit. Backups are never paywalled. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does CPSC recall checking work?

HomeRecall checks your assets against the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) recall database. To be checked, each asset must have a brand/make entered. The app covers recalls from January 1, 2000 to present. Adding a model number improves matching accuracy. This feature is for informational purposes only — always verify recalls at cpsc.gov.

Do I need an internet connection?

Most organizing features work without an internet connection. Internet access is needed for CPSC recall checks, optional Apple Maps previews, subscription purchases or restoration, and opening external links.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device to manage or cancel.
