Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built Your House, Clearly (the "App") as a local-first home maintenance and records app with an optional one-time Premium purchase. This policy explains what stays on your device, when limited information is sent to outside services, and the choices available to you.

1. Local App Data

The App stores your records in its local app container on your device. These records can include property details and addresses, asset information, maintenance tasks and logs, photos, receipts, manuals, warranties, other documents, generated map images, and saved recall results.

Your House, Clearly does not require an account. We do not operate an app cloud database, cloud-sync service, or analytics backend. The App does not automatically transmit your local records to Tyche Ventures LLC. We receive information or files only if you voluntarily send them to us, such as in a support email.

2. CPSC Recall Checks

Recall checking is an internet feature. When a check runs—such as a periodic check while you use the App, a check after saving an asset, or a manual refresh—the App sends the asset's entered manufacturer/brand and, when available, model as search terms to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's SaferProducts.gov API. It does not send your property address, photos, documents, Apple ID, or an app account identifier to that API.

As with a normal internet request, the CPSC service may receive technical information such as your IP address and request metadata. CPSC's published policy explains that it uses technical visit information for aggregate analytics and can retain web and cookie-log information for up to three years. Your House, Clearly uses a temporary recall-search session, requests uncached responses, and does not store or resend cookies set by the CPSC service. CPSC still controls information it receives and its retention. Review the CPSC Privacy Policy for details. Recall responses and possible matches are saved locally in the App. Tyche Ventures LLC does not receive the search terms, technical request data, or responses.

For App Store privacy disclosure purposes, recall terms are described as Search History used for App Functionality. Technical request data that CPSC may retain and analyze is described conservatively as Other Data used for App Functionality and Analytics. Both categories are disclosed as not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

3. Apple Services

  • Apple Maps: If you ask the App to generate a map preview, the property address you entered is submitted through Apple's geocoding and map services. The resulting preview is saved locally. Tyche Ventures LLC does not receive that address or preview. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • App Store and StoreKit: Apple processes the optional one-time Premium purchase, restoration, and billing. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple ID credentials. Apple may provide the App with entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock Premium.
  • Camera, Photos, and document scanning: With your permission, the App can use system interfaces to capture or select files. The App saves selected content locally; it does not upload those files to Tyche Ventures LLC.

4. Notifications

If you grant permission, Your House, Clearly schedules local notifications for maintenance tasks, warranty dates, and newly found high-confidence possible recall matches. These alerts and reminders are generated by your device and do not use a Tyche Ventures LLC push-notification server.

5. Export, Backup, and Sharing

The App can create a local .yourhouseclearly backup archive containing your properties, assets, tasks, maintenance logs, document metadata, and included property or document files. Your House, Clearly can also import compatible legacy .placeworthy archives created under the former Placeworthy working name, .dwelllore archives created under the former DwellLore name, and older .homerecall archives created under the former HomeRecall name. New exports use the .yourhouseclearly format. Saved recall matches and recall-check history are not included. After you restore an archive, the App can generate new possible recall matches by checking the CPSC service again as you use the App, when an internet connection and the required asset information are available.

Your House, Clearly backup archives are not encrypted by the App. Store them only in a secure destination you trust. Nothing is sent merely because a backup archive or PDF maintenance report is created. If you use the iOS share sheet, you choose the destination, such as Files, AirDrop, Messages, or email, and that destination's privacy and security practices apply.

Your House, Clearly does not provide its own cloud backup. Exported copies and device or system backups remain wherever you choose to store them and are not removed when you delete the corresponding record in the App.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Your House, Clearly contains no advertising, in-app analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not build advertising profiles, sell your app data, or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. This does not change the separate CPSC processing described above when you use recall checking.

7. Storage and Security

Local records are protected by the iOS app sandbox and the security controls you enable on your device. No method of storage is risk-free. Keep iOS updated, protect your device with a strong passcode, and maintain current exports of records you cannot afford to lose.

8. Retention and Deletion

You control the records stored in the App and can edit or delete them. Uninstalling Your House, Clearly removes its local app container under normal iOS behavior. Copies you previously exported, shared, or included in a device or system backup may remain at those destinations until you delete them there.

Tyche Ventures LLC has no automatically transmitted Your House, Clearly app-data server copy to delete. Voluntary support communications are handled as described below. Requests sent to Apple or CPSC are governed by those services' retention policies.

9. Your Choices

  • Access and correction: View and edit your records in the App.
  • Deletion: Delete individual records or uninstall the App.
  • Portability: Export a .yourhouseclearly archive.
  • Permissions: Change camera, photo-library, and notification access in iOS Settings.
  • Network features: You can choose not to generate map previews or initiate manual recall checks. Periodic recall checks require an internet connection and asset manufacturer data.

10. Children

The App is intended for adults managing homes or household records. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly operate a service that collects personal information from children.

11. Support Communications

If you email support, Tyche Ventures LLC and its email service provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to you, diagnose reported issues, protect the service, and maintain records reasonably needed for legal or business purposes.

We retain support communications only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, subject to applicable law and ordinary backup retention. Do not send a Your House, Clearly backup archive or sensitive documents unless they are necessary for your support request and you accept the risks of transmitting them by email.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the App or release notes when appropriate.
