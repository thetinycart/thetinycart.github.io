A practical home record

Remember the details your house cannot.

Your House, Clearly connects the everyday work of homeownership: what you own, when it needs attention, what coverage remains, and where the paperwork lives.

01

Maintenance that repeats

Start from 100+ built-in templates or create your own one-time and recurring tasks. Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

02

Asset details that stay findable

Record manufacturer, model, serial number, purchase details, warranty dates, notes, photos, and related files for appliances and home systems.

03

Warranty dates in one view

Review active and upcoming coverage dates across recorded assets, then open the supporting receipt, warranty, or manual when you need it.

04

Documents beside the record

Attach photos, scans, and PDFs to a property or asset instead of relying on a separate folder whose context is easy to forget.

05

Possible CPSC recall matches

Use the manufacturer and model you enter to search CPSC data from January 1, 2000 onward. Review possible matches and verify them with the official notice.

06

Logs, reports, and backups

Keep a maintenance history, create a shareable PDF report, and export a restorable local backup without putting those tools behind Premium.
