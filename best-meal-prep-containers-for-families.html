Meal prepping saves the average family 4-5 hours per week and cuts grocery spending by up to 25%. But it only works if your containers don't leak in the fridge, stain after one use of tomato sauce, or lose their lids in the dishwasher abyss.

Here are the containers that actually hold up to family life.

Glass vs. Plastic vs. Stainless Steel

Glass: Best for reheating. Won't stain or absorb odors. Heavy and breakable — not great for kids to carry.

Plastic: Lightweight, cheap, kid-friendly. Look for BPA-free and don't microwave with the lid on.

Stainless steel: Most durable, eco-friendly. Can't microwave. Great for cold meals and snacks.

Meal Prep Tips for Families

  • Sunday prep, weekday peace. Spend 1-2 hours on Sunday prepping lunches and snacks for the whole week.
  • Label everything. A piece of painter's tape with the day written on it saves morning confusion.
  • Plan before you prep. Use an app like Kids Meal Planner to map out the week, then generate a shopping list automatically.
  • Freeze extras. Most prepped meals last 3-4 days in the fridge. Freeze the rest.
