Frequently Asked Questions

How does CartKind work?

Scan any barcode at the store. CartKind looks up the product's ingredients and checks them against your personal rules. You get a clear verdict: pass, caution, or fail.

Where does the ingredient data come from?

CartKind uses Open Food Facts, an open-source, community-maintained product database. Data may occasionally be incomplete or outdated — always verify by reading the physical label.

Can I customize which ingredients to flag?

Yes. In the Rules tab, you can set each ingredient category to Ignore, Caution, or Fail. Premium users can also add custom ingredient triggers and categories.

What does Premium include?

  • Unlimited scan history (free tier: last 30 scans)
  • Weekly insights dashboard
  • Custom ingredient triggers and categories

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → CartKind. You can change or cancel your plan from there. Apple handles all billing.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your rules, preferences, and scan history are stored locally on your device. We have no analytics, no tracking, and no server-side storage. See our Privacy Policy for details.

A product scanned incorrectly — what should I do?

Open Food Facts is community-maintained, so some entries may be incomplete. You can contribute corrections directly at openfoodfacts.org, and CartKind will pick up the updated data on your next scan.
