How does CartKind work?

Scan or manually enter a barcode and CartKind looks up the product's ingredients, checks them against your personal rules, and shows Aligned, Review, or Not aligned. These labels describe your configured preferences—not whether a product is medically safe.

What if I can't scan the barcode?

You can enter a barcode manually. If lookup still cannot produce a usable result, CartKind also supports manual ingredient entry.

Where does the ingredient data come from?

CartKind queries Open Food Facts directly first. Only when Open Food Facts reports no matching product can CartKind use a Tyche Ventures relay hosted on Cloudflare Workers to query USDA FoodData Central. Product data can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate, so always verify the physical label. See the Privacy Policy for network and retention details.

Can I customize which ingredients to flag?

Yes. In the Rules tab, you can set ingredient categories to Ignore, Warn me, or Avoid. Premium adds custom ingredient triggers and custom categories.

What does Premium include?

  • Weekly Insights with verdict and category trends, common rule matches, repeat product patterns, and drill-down lists
  • Custom ingredient triggers
  • Custom ingredient categories
  • Custom rules applied to new evaluations while Premium access is active

Saved scan history remains available in the free app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → CartKind. Apple handles billing, plan changes, and cancellation.

Is my data private?

Rules, custom settings, and saved history are stored locally. CartKind does not require an account or include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Barcode lookup still sends the barcode and ordinary request metadata to Open Food Facts and, after a catalog miss, may use the Tyche Ventures/Cloudflare USDA relay. See the Privacy Policy for the exact flow, possible short-lived logs, and deletion details.

Why does a result open an external source link?

When available, CartKind can open the product's Open Food Facts or USDA FoodData Central source page in your browser. The destination's privacy policy and terms apply.

A product scanned incorrectly. What should I do?

Product data comes from third-party sources and may occasionally be incomplete or outdated. Always verify the physical label. If the product came from Open Food Facts, you can contribute corrections at openfoodfacts.org, and CartKind can pick up updated data on a later scan. If the result came from USDA FoodData Central, use the source link to review the USDA record.
