Always verify the physical label

CartKind is an informational shopping reference, not medical advice or an allergy-safety tool. Aligned, Review, and Not aligned compare available ingredient text with your rules; they do not establish that a product is safe. Product databases can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Read the package label every time and consult a qualified professional about allergies, intolerances, or medical dietary needs.

How does CartKind work?

Scan or manually enter a barcode and CartKind looks up available product ingredients, compares them with your personal rules, and shows Aligned, Review, or Not aligned. These labels reflect only the rules you configured and the ingredient text returned by the source.

Where does the product data come from?

CartKind queries Open Food Facts directly first. Only after Open Food Facts reports no matching product may CartKind send the barcode to a Tyche Ventures relay hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which queries USDA FoodData Central. A result identifies its source when available.

If neither source returns usable ingredients, you can enter ingredients manually. Source data and manual entries still require verification against the physical package label.

What if the barcode will not scan?

Make sure CartKind has camera permission in iOS Settings, hold the barcode inside the scanner guide, and try brighter, even lighting. You can also enter the barcode manually. Camera frames are processed on the device for barcode recognition; CartKind does not store, record, or transmit them.

Can I customize which ingredients to flag?

Yes. In the Rules tab, you can set ingredient categories to Ignore, Warn me, or Avoid. Premium adds custom ingredient triggers and custom categories. Rules are personal preferences, not diagnoses or medical safety standards.

What does Premium include?

  • Weekly Insights with verdict and category trends, common rule matches, repeat-product patterns, and drill-down lists
  • Custom ingredient triggers
  • Custom ingredient categories
  • Custom rules applied to new evaluations while Premium access is active

Saved scan history remains available in the free app.

How do I manage, restore, or cancel a subscription?

Apple processes CartKind subscriptions through StoreKit. Use the restore-purchases option in CartKind when needed. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings, choose your Apple ID, select Subscriptions, and choose CartKind. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation, and payment information.

Is my data private?

Rules, custom settings, saved history, product records, and preferences are stored in CartKind's local app container. CartKind does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs. Apple-managed backups may include local data, depending on your settings.

A barcode lookup does leave the device. It goes directly to Open Food Facts first. After an Open Food Facts catalog miss, it may go to the Tyche Ventures/Cloudflare USDA relay. The relay can process ordinary request metadata and, if Workers logging is enabled, limited invocation logs may be retained for up to seven days. The Privacy Policy explains the flow, recipients, retention, and deletion choices.

Why does a result open an external source link?

When available, CartKind can open the product's Open Food Facts or USDA FoodData Central record in your browser. This happens only when you choose the link. The destination's privacy policy and terms apply.

A product looks incorrect. What should I do?

First, rely on the physical package label—not the database result. If the source is Open Food Facts, you can review or contribute corrections at openfoodfacts.org. If the source is USDA FoodData Central, use the source link to review the USDA record. CartKind may show updated data on a later lookup.
