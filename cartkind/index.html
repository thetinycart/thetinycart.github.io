🛒 CartKind

Your clean rules. Your cart.

CartKind is a calm, minimal barcode scanner that checks product ingredients against your personal rules. Set which ingredients matter to you — seed oils, artificial colors, preservatives, sweeteners — and scan anything at the store. One clear verdict: pass, caution, or fail. No alarmist language. No nutrition scores. Just your rules, your way.
