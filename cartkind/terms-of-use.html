2. Description of the service

CartKind scans or accepts product barcodes, retrieves available product information, compares available ingredient text with user-defined rules, and presents an Aligned, Review, or Not aligned result. You can also enter ingredients manually. Optional Premium features include Insights, custom ingredient triggers, custom categories, and custom rules applied to new evaluations while Premium access is active.

Results reflect only the rules you configured and the source or ingredient text available at the time. CartKind does not diagnose an allergy or intolerance, verify cross-contact, or certify that a product is suitable or safe.

3. Third-party product data

CartKind queries Open Food Facts directly first. Only after Open Food Facts reports no matching product may CartKind send the barcode to a Tyche Ventures relay hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which queries USDA FoodData Central. A result identifies its source when available.

Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central are independent sources. Tyche Ventures does not guarantee that either catalog or any result is complete, accurate, current, or available. Product formulas and labels can change. You are responsible for reading and verifying the physical product label before making a purchase, consumption, allergy, or medical decision.
