Description of service

CartKind is an informational shopping reference app. It scans or accepts product barcodes, compares available ingredient text with user-defined rules, and presents an Aligned, Review, or Not aligned result based on the rules you configured. These labels are preference comparisons, not safety determinations. CartKind is not a medical device and does not identify whether a food is safe for an allergy, intolerance, or medical condition.

Third-party product data

CartKind queries Open Food Facts directly as its primary source. Only after Open Food Facts reports no matching product may CartKind use a Tyche Ventures relay hosted on Cloudflare Workers to query USDA FoodData Central. A result is identified by its source; CartKind does not guarantee that either catalog is complete, current, or accurate. You are responsible for reading and verifying the physical product label before making food decisions.
