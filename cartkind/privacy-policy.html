Privacy Policy

Effective: March 3, 2026

Overview

CartKind ("the App") is developed and published by Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "us," "our"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data CartKind collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

CartKind is designed as a privacy-first, offline-first application. Here is what happens with your data:

  • Barcode scans: When you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (an open-source product database) to retrieve ingredient information. No personal information is included in this request.
  • Ingredient rules and preferences: Your category settings, severity levels, and custom rules are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Scan history: Your scan history is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Camera access: The app requests camera access solely for barcode scanning. Camera images are processed on-device in real time and are not stored, recorded, or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

  • Open Food Facts API: When scanning a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product data. Open Food Facts is a nonprofit, open-source project. Their privacy policy is available at openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. We have no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Storage and Security

All app data (scan history, preferences, rules) is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed. We do not maintain any server-side database of user information.

Children's Privacy

CartKind does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or any personal information to use.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us
