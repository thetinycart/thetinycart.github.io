Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Overview

Tyche Ventures LLC built CartKind as a free app with optional Premium features. CartKind is local-first and does not require an account. This policy explains what stays on your device and the limited information sent when you use product lookup, purchases, or external source links.

Age and children's privacy

CartKind is intended as a shopping-reference tool for parents and guardians and is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask users to create an account or provide a name or email address. A parent or guardian should decide whether the app is appropriate for a child to use with supervision.

Information stored on your device

Your rules, custom triggers, custom categories, saved scan history, product records, and app preferences are stored in CartKind's local app container. Tyche Ventures LLC does not operate a CartKind account system or automatically upload those local records to a server-side user database.

You can delete individual history items in the app and remove custom rules through the available controls. Deleting CartKind normally removes its local app container. Copies included in Apple-managed device backups may remain until those backups are deleted or expire under Apple's practices.

Product lookup and network data

When you scan or manually enter a barcode, CartKind sends the barcode over HTTPS to Open Food Facts directly from your device. Open Food Facts also receives ordinary request metadata, such as your IP address, request time, and CartKind's identifying User-Agent. CartKind does not send camera images, your saved rules, or your scan history with this request. Open Food Facts describes its own retention and use of IP and log data—including security, technical analysis, usage statistics, and product-popularity measurement—in its privacy policy.

Only when Open Food Facts returns no matching product, CartKind may send the barcode to the Tyche Ventures USDA relay at usda-api.tycheventuresllc.com. The relay runs on Cloudflare Workers and forwards the barcode search to USDA FoodData Central. The relay and Cloudflare can process the barcode together with ordinary technical request metadata, such as an IP address, timestamp, request URL, and headers. The relay does not receive your camera image, saved rules, custom triggers, custom categories, or full scan history, and its code does not intentionally forward your device IP address to USDA.

If Cloudflare Workers logging is enabled, invocation logs may include the request URL containing the barcode and related technical metadata. Cloudflare currently documents approximately three days of retention for Workers Free logs and up to seven days for Workers Paid logs. Because plan and logging configuration can change, Tyche Ventures LLC conservatively treats these proxy logs as potentially retained for up to seven days. They are not used to create a CartKind account, build an advertising profile, or track you across apps or websites. See Cloudflare Workers logging and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Camera access

CartKind requests camera access only when you choose barcode scanning. Camera frames are processed on-device in real time by Apple's barcode metadata scanner. CartKind stores the decoded barcode, not camera images; frames are not stored, recorded, or transmitted.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts: Receives direct barcode lookups and ordinary request metadata as described above. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers and USDA FoodData Central: The Tyche Ventures relay is used only after an Open Food Facts catalog miss. Cloudflare hosts the relay, and USDA supplies the fallback product record. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and USDA privacy information.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple. Tyche Ventures LLC does not receive or store your payment-card details or Apple ID credentials.
  • External source links: Only when you choose a source-reference link, CartKind opens that destination in your browser. The destination's privacy policy and terms apply.

Data storage and security

CartKind uses Apple's local-storage frameworks for app records. Local data benefits from the device's passcode, biometric security, and built-in protections. No storage or transmission method is risk-free. Network requests are encrypted in transit with HTTPS, while recipients and infrastructure providers process them under their own security and privacy practices.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

CartKind does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs. Tyche Ventures LLC does not sell personal data or use lookup requests to build advertising profiles. Operational network providers may generate the technical logs described above, and Open Food Facts states that it can use request logs for technical analysis, usage statistics, and product-popularity measurement.

What can leave the device

The following information may leave the device during normal use:

  • A scanned or manually entered barcode plus ordinary request metadata sent to Open Food Facts.
  • After an Open Food Facts catalog miss, the barcode and request metadata sent to the Tyche Ventures/Cloudflare relay, which then sends the barcode query to USDA.
  • Browser requests you initiate by tapping external source links.
  • StoreKit purchase and subscription communications with Apple.
  • Apple-managed backup data, depending on your device settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in CartKind or remove the app, subject to Apple-managed backups. Tyche Ventures LLC does not maintain a separate CartKind user-profile database. Proxy invocation logs, if enabled, may remain for up to seven days as described above. Open Food Facts, Cloudflare, USDA, Apple, and any external site apply their own retention practices.

Health and allergy disclaimer

CartKind is an informational shopping reference tool only. Product data can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Always read the physical product label and consult a qualified professional for allergy, intolerance, or medical dietary concerns.
