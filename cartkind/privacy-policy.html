Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC ("we," "our," or "us") built the CartKind app (the "App") as a free application with optional premium features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Age Requirement

The App is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

All data you create in the App — including scan history, ingredient rules, category severities, custom triggers, and custom categories — is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data. We do not have servers that receive, store, or process your information.

3. Camera Access

The App requests access to your device's camera solely for barcode scanning. Camera images are processed on-device in real time to detect barcodes. Images are not stored, recorded, or transmitted. No photos are taken or saved during scanning. You can revoke camera access at any time through your device's Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Open Food Facts API: When you scan a barcode, the App may send only the barcode number to the Open Food Facts API (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product and ingredient information. No personal information is sent — only the barcode number. No device identifiers, user IDs, or location data are included. Open Food Facts is a free, open-source, nonprofit database. Their privacy policy is available at openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

USDA FoodData Central: If Open Food Facts is unavailable or does not return a usable product match, the App may send the barcode number to USDA FoodData Central to retrieve branded food information, including ingredient data when available. No personal information is included in this request. USDA's privacy and data policies are available at usda.gov/privacy-policy.

Apple App Store / StoreKit: If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional external links: If you choose to open an external product or shopping link from the App, that destination opens in your browser and is governed by the third party's own privacy practices.

5. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is protected by:

  • Your device's passcode/biometric security (Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Apple's built-in data encryption (Data Protection)
  • The App does not create any user accounts or cloud backups

If you delete the App, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

6. Data Breach Notification

Because all data is stored locally on your device and we do not operate servers or databases that store user information, the risk of a data breach from our systems is effectively zero. The only network request the App makes is the barcode lookup, which transmits no personal data. In the unlikely event that a vulnerability in the App is discovered that could compromise locally stored data, we will promptly disclose the issue via an App update and on our website, and provide guidance on how to protect your information.

7. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, location, or any other activity within the App. We do not display advertisements. We do not use cookies. We do not create user profiles.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. We will never sell your data, and we have never sold user data. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts and, when needed, USDA FoodData Central (when you explicitly scan a product)
  • Optional browser requests you initiate by tapping external links

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full and complete control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App at all times
  • Correction: You can edit any rules or settings at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual scan records or uninstall the App to remove all data permanently

For California residents: Under the CCPA, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. Since we collect no personal information and sell no data, these rights are inherently satisfied.

For EU/EEA residents: Under the GDPR, you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Since all data is local to your device and we process no personal data, these rights are inherently satisfied.

10. Health and Allergy Disclaimer

The App does not verify food safety, allergen presence, or nutritional adequacy. The App is an informational reference tool only. Ingredient data comes from third-party sources that may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Users are solely responsible for reading physical product labels, verifying ingredients, and consulting healthcare professionals regarding allergies, intolerances, or medical dietary needs. Do not rely solely on the App for allergy or food safety decisions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
