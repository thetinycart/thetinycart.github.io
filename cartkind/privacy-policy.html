Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

Tyche Ventures LLC built CartKind as a free app with optional premium features. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Age and children's privacy

CartKind does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not require an account and we do not receive personal data to our own servers, CartKind can be used as a personal shopping reference with parent or guardian guidance where appropriate.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers.

Rules, saved scan history, custom triggers, and custom categories are stored locally on the device.

Camera access

CartKind requests camera access only so the app can scan barcodes. Camera images are processed on-device in real time to detect barcodes. Images are not stored, recorded, or transmitted.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts: When you scan or search a barcode, CartKind may send the barcode number to Open Food Facts to retrieve product and ingredient information. No personal information is included in this request. Their privacy policy is available at openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • USDA FoodData Central: If Open Food Facts does not return a usable match, CartKind may send the barcode number to USDA FoodData Central to retrieve branded food information. No personal information is included in this request. USDA privacy information is available at usda.gov/privacy-policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Subscription purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.
  • Optional external links: If you choose to open a source reference or shopping link from the app, that destination opens in your browser and is governed by the third party's own privacy practices.

Data storage and security

CartKind stores its local app data on the device using Apple's frameworks. Local data benefits from the device's passcode, biometric security, and Apple's built-in device protections.

CartKind does not operate its own cloud backend, user-account system, or server-side user database.

Local app data may still be included in Apple-managed device backups, such as iCloud Backup or computer backups, depending on the user's device settings and Apple's platform behavior.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

CartKind does not use analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, or tracking SDKs. We do not build user profiles and we do not sell user data.

What can leave the device

The main non-personal data that may leave the device during normal use is:

  • barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts and, when needed, USDA FoodData Central
  • browser requests you initiate by tapping external links
  • Apple-managed backup data if the user has enabled device backups

Health and allergy disclaimer

CartKind is an informational shopping reference tool only. Product data can be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Always read the physical product label and consult a qualified professional for allergy, intolerance, or medical dietary concerns.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the revised version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact support@tycheventuresllc.com.
