Templates are planning ideas—not safety classifications.

Review every item for ingredients, package labels, cross-contact, portions, and caregiver rules. A template name does not certify that a plan is allergen-free, nutritionally complete, medically appropriate, or suitable for a particular child.

Privacy and safety

Clear boundaries for a family planning tool.

Kids Meal Planner organizes information. It does not make food-safety or nutrition decisions for you.

Local planning data

Profiles, meal plans, pantry items, and shopping-list content are stored in the app. Apple-managed backups and files you export can create additional copies.

Barcode lookup connects

A scanned code and standard network metadata go to Open Food Facts. It may retain IP and request logs under its policies, and returned product data may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Third-party actions are explicit

Apple processes subscriptions. Optional Amazon affiliate links open outside the app. PDFs leave the app only when you choose to save or share them.

Package labels control

Planning notes, templates, and barcode results are not verified for allergens, nutrition, or food safety. Read the privacy policy.

Important: Kids Meal Planner does not identify allergies or determine food safety.

Always check ingredients and package labels, account for cross-contact and individual needs, and consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions.
