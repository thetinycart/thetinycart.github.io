Your table. Your records.

No TallyKeeps account, ads or automatic sending. Records stay on your device. The current beta does not include cloud sync, editable backups, online multiplayer, automatic game-rule engines or payments between players.

Scoring guides identify particular variants; they are not complete rules or publisher endorsements. Check the guide and your agreed house rules before play. The support guide explains the current build’s formats and storage limits.

Currently in TestFlight

TallyKeeps is being tested with invited reviewers on iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. It is not publicly released yet. An invitation and an available build in Apple TestFlight are required.

The beta has no subscription or in-app purchase. Public pricing has not been finalized. This page does not sell access or promise unlimited storage.
