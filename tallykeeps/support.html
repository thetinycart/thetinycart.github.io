TallyKeeps · TestFlight help

Back to the table.

A quick guide to the iPhone and iPad beta. Updated September 8, 2026.

Need a hand?

Email support@tycheventuresllc.com with your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and the exact message. Leave private names, photos and meeting details out of screenshots unless needed.

Start a game

Open Play → New game. Choose a game or Custom game, then select a group or add today’s players. The Group menu also offers Create group; groups are optional. Most setups support 2–6 players; the selected guide shows any narrower limit, such as exactly four for Hearts.

In build 8, the 45 guides are grouped into card, board, dice, domino and party games, alphabetically within each category. Search also recognizes familiar alternative names. Guides describe specific scoring variants, not complete game rules or publisher-endorsed products. Custom is available for other games and house rules.

Enter, correct and finish scores

Follow the chosen guide’s entry method. For round or turn points, calculate that entry’s points—not the running total. A Final score only guide instead takes the completed game’s total once. Final-winner and finishing-place guides record the result after you apply the physical game’s rules and tiebreaks; they do not store a numerical breakdown. Enter 0 when appropriate; a blank score is still missing. Negative point scores are supported.

Save the entry to update the sheet. Tap an earlier entry to correct it; reopen scoring first if the game is finished. Follow your guide’s finish instructions and tap Finish yourself. Reaching a target does not end play automatically. Specialized boards, category scorecards, dealer outcomes, card counting and cash settlement are not provided. Poker is limited to completed tournament finishing places, not chip or money tracking.

Keep your regular players together

People can have a local photo or emoji. The optional Contacts picker copies only the selected name. Groups remember regular players, and a game night can contain several games. Rankings compare wins, tied first places and win rates—not the sum of unrelated games’ points. Matching names are never assumed to belong to the same person.

Save a custom game for next time

In TestFlight 1.0 (8) or later, Custom game offers Save for next time. Leave it on to save the name, scoring method and optional photo or emoji when you start the match. A dice image is used if you do not choose artwork. Players, groups and scores are never attached to the reusable game. Turn it off for a one-off match.

Next time, find it under Your saved games in New game, then choose the group or players for that occasion. Create a saved game lets you prepare one without starting a match. Manage saved games edits the existing game’s name, scoring or artwork without making another copy or changing past results. In build 7, the earlier setup manager is still under Settings → My game setups; update to build 8 for the new route and pictures. A build must be available in your TestFlight before you can install it.

Practice and results

  • Practice without saving: Explore an example appears until first use. After opening it, find it again under Settings → Explore the practice example. Practice never counts as a real game or leaderboard result.
  • Overall standings: finished games count even without a group. New players are saved in People. Choose an existing player on the next game to accumulate their record. Older unlinked players appear separately by game rather than being merged by name.
  • Groups after play: Create group with these players keeps the people from a finished game together for next time. It does not rewrite the original game’s group history. Groups can have a photo or emoji. A preferred saved game is optional.

For a tied placement, use competition places: 1st, 1st, 3rd. For win/loss, select a winner, joint winners, or a draw for everyone. Save the result, then Finish to include it in standings. Reopen scoring before changing a finished result.

Share and preserve your records

Review invitation or result text before choosing a sharing app and recipients. Nothing is sent automatically. Sharing results does not create an editable backup, synchronize devices or track replies.

Records stay on this device. Keep the app installed: there is no TallyKeeps cloud recovery or in-app archive backup. Current limits are 100 games, 100 rounds per game, 200 people, 50 groups, 100 saved setups and 100 game nights. The combined 4 MiB storage limit can be reached sooner, particularly with profile photos.

If saving or opening records fails, preserve your draft and note the exact message. Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step. Contact support before changing existing records.

About the beta
