BingoKeeps: Photo Bingo

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

Tyche Ventures LLC develops BingoKeeps, a Mac app for making picture-bingo cards and caller sheets. This policy describes the app's specific practices.

Your games stay local. BingoKeeps has no Tyche account, app-operated cloud synchronization, ads, tracking or analytics SDK. It does not automatically send your games or photos to Tyche Ventures or an AI service.

Photos, labels and saved games

BingoKeeps stores the working draft and named games on your Mac. They can contain imported photo copies, labels, game names, print settings and the information needed to recreate card arrangements.

You choose files through the Mac's file picker. BingoKeeps creates normalized image copies without changing your originals or copying their source EXIF/GPS metadata. A filename can become the initial picture label. Removing metadata does not anonymize faces, visible information or labels; check those before sharing. Built-in templates do not require importing personal photos.

Printing, exports and other services

PDFs contain the game's pictures and labels. When you print, the macOS printing system and the destination you choose receive the print content. Saving to a cloud-synced folder or later sharing a PDF can create copies outside your Mac. Those destinations, backups and recipients have their own practices.

Apple processes App Store purchases and distribution under Apple's Privacy Policy. BingoKeeps does not request payment-card information or your Apple Account password. Opening a website or sending email uses your browser or email service.

Retention and deletion

Working edits, named saved games and exported PDFs are separate copies. Removing a picture from your working game does not remove it from an earlier saved snapshot or PDF; Save Changes updates the corresponding named game. Exports and backups remain until removed at their destinations. Removing the application may leave local app data behind.

Contact support for guidance before removing all local data. We cannot remotely access, erase or recover your local games.

If you contact us

We receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and investigate, and retain them only as needed for support, security, necessary business records or legal obligations. Avoid sending private photos or other sensitive information. You may request deletion of support information we control, subject to lawful retention needs.

Protecting your information

Protect access to your Mac and exported files. No storage or transmission is completely secure. Adults preparing games should obtain appropriate permission before using or sharing someone else's photo, especially a child's.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Tyche Ventures LLC at support@tycheventuresllc.com. We will update this page when these practices materially change.
