Your pictures.

Turn familiar faces, favorite places or colorful illustrations into a bingo game for your next gathering. Name the pictures, make your cards, and print a pack to play together.

From pictures to a ready-to-print game

01 / Make it yours

Choose pictures and names

Import JPEG, PNG or HEIC pictures, or start with an illustrated template. Edit every label and use 16–40 pictures in a game.

02 / Prepare the pack

Cards and callers, together

Make 1–50 player cards, with two 4×4 cards per page. Matching caller sheets include your whole picture collection. Choose Letter or A4.

03 / Gather again

Print now. Keep for later.

Print directly from the app or save a PDF to share. Keep a named game in My Games and reopen it without searching for a project file.

Local by design

Your pictures and editable games stay on your Mac. No Tyche account, cloud sync, ads or analytics SDK. Exported PDFs contain your pictures and labels; you decide where to save or share them.

How your information is handled

Simple to buy

One upfront App Store purchase includes all templates and saved-game features. There is no subscription, in-app unlock or app-imposed saved-game count limit. Available Mac storage still applies.

The Mac release is in preparation. iPhone and iPad versions are not currently available.

Made for gathering

Four matching pictures in a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line wins. Every card has a distinct arrangement; ties are possible. Review the pack and printer settings before printing, and have an adult help with cutting.
