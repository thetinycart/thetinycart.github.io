BingoKeeps: Photo Bingo

Let's get your game ready.

Help for the Mac app · Updated September 6, 2026

Contact support

Email support@tycheventuresllc.com with your app version, macOS version, Mac model, what you tried and the exact error. For printing issues, include paper size and printer model. Please omit private photos, children's information and sensitive filenames unless necessary. We cannot see your local games automatically.

Start your first game

  1. Choose Use my photos or Choose template. A game uses 16–40 pictures, with 16 on each player card.
  2. Review the picture labels and name your game. Save Game keeps it in My Games.
  3. Choose 1–50 player cards and Letter or A4 paper, then Review print pack. Caller sheets include the whole picture pool.
  4. Choose Print for the Mac print dialog, or Save PDF for a printable file you can keep or share.

Do I have to save a PDF before printing?

No. After reviewing the pack, choose Print. Confirm the printer, landscape orientation, matching paper and scaling before submitting the job. Canceling the dialog leaves your game and preview available. App confirmation means the macOS print workflow completed, not that paper necessarily emerged from the printer.

How do I save and reopen a game?

Your working draft autosaves locally. Save Game keeps a named version in My Games. Save Changes updates that game, including a changed title. Click its named card in My Games to edit it or print the same arrangements again. My Games is not a list of exported PDFs. Switching games asks about working changes not yet kept in My Games.

Why is a picture missing from a card?

Each card contains 16 pictures. With a larger pool, a particular card uses only some of them; caller sheets include all pictures. With exactly 16 pictures, every card contains that same set in a different arrangement. Four in a row wins; ties are possible.

Why can't I review the print pack?

Check for fewer than 16 pictures, missing labels, overlong text or an import error. Supported imports are JPEG, PNG and HEIC up to 40 megapixels. Exact normalized duplicates are skipped. A low-resolution warning means the printed picture may look soft; use a clearer original if possible.

What if saving fails?

Keep the app open with your game, check available storage and retry using the message shown. Do not discard changes, reinstall or remove app data as the first troubleshooting step. Contact support if the message persists. We cannot restore local games from a Tyche cloud account because there is none.

How do I remove personal pictures?

Remove the picture from the working game; use Save Changes if the corresponding named game should also change. Earlier PDFs, other saved games and backups may still contain it. For complete local-data removal, contact support for guidance; deleting the app alone may leave its data behind.

Is there a subscription or Restore Purchases button?

No. BingoKeeps is paid upfront through the App Store, without in-app purchases. For download, billing or refund help, use your App Store purchase history and Apple's support guidance. Your purchase is not a cloud backup of your games.

Does it work on iPhone or iPad?

The current release is being prepared for Mac. iPhone and iPad versions are not currently available. Cross-device game synchronization is not included.
