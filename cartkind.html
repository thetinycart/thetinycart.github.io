Privacy and limits

Local-first, with the network flow spelled out.

Rules and saved history stay on your device. Barcode lookup uses third-party product catalogs, so the source and limitations matter.

No CartKind account

Rules, custom settings, and saved evaluations are stored locally. Apple-managed backups can include app data depending on your device settings.

Open Food Facts first

A barcode lookup sends the barcode and standard network metadata to Open Food Facts. Its returned data may be incomplete or inaccurate.

USDA after a catalog miss

Only after Open Food Facts reports no match, a Tyche Ventures relay requests USDA FoodData Central results. The relay may retain limited security logs for up to seven days.

Sources stay visible

Open Food Facts and USDA references can be opened when you choose. Read the full privacy policy.

Important: this is not an allergy or food-safety check.

CartKind is an informational shopping reference—not medical, allergy, nutrition, or food-safety advice. It does not identify allergens or determine whether a product is safe. Product information can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Always read the physical label and consult a qualified professional for medical or allergy decisions.
