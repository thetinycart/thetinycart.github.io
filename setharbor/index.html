Built around operational truth

Know what is planned, where it is, and what happened next.

SetHarbor separates availability, physical location, condition, ownership, and lifecycle so one status does not have to stand in for everything.

01

Plan rooms with exact pieces

Create a property, set its dates and rooms, record access context, and reserve specific inventory for the plan.

02

Keep field moves focused

Work through pull, load, install, destage, return, move, receive, and audit tasks without losing the route or the next expected action.

03

Scan—or enter the ID

Use the camera for your own opaque internal QR labels, or enter an item ID manually when scanning is not the right choice.

04

Return with condition in view

Inspect returning pieces, record condition, surface exceptions, and preserve the movement history behind the current state.

05

06

Export when you choose

Create an inventory CSV only when you initiate it, review the file, and choose its destination through the system share sheet.

One connected operating loop

From warehouse plan to property—and back.

The workflow follows physical inventory rather than asking the team to translate a generic database into staging work.

1 · Plan

Create the property and rooms, set dates, and reserve the exact pieces intended for the installation.

2 · Pull & load

Confirm pieces as they move from storage to the vehicle, using an internal QR label or manual ID.

3 · Install & adjust

Record what reaches each property and room, including moves, direct rolls, and exceptions.

4 · Return & inspect

Destage, receive, inspect condition, and reconcile the piece with its next physical location.

Your existing records are not taken away if Studio changes.

If a Studio entitlement expires or cannot be verified, existing records and active work remain accessible. SetHarbor limits only net-new expansion above the evaluation allowance until Studio is active again. Review the subscription terms for billing and renewal details.

Private by structure

A local workspace, with deliberate ways out.

The current first-release design does not require an account or send your operational workspace to a Tyche Ventures server.

Local and offline

Projects, inventory, locations, conditions, notes, and movement history are kept in the app's local container. Core workflows can be used without a network connection.

No surveillance layer

SetHarbor contains no ads, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking and does not create a Tyche account.

Camera and photos are optional

The camera can read internal QR labels, and a chosen photo can identify a piece. Manual entry remains available; captured content stays local.

Two deliberate connections

Apple StoreKit handles subscriptions. A CSV leaves the app only when the owner initiates an export and chooses a destination.
